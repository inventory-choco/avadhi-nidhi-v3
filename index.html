Monthly 105 AED Contribution Structure

Every month each member pays 105 AED. From this, 100 AED goes into their personal monthly savings balance, and 5 AED is deducted automatically into the SahayaNidhi Charity Fund. Even if a member misses a monthly savings contribution, the compulsory 5 AED SahayaNidhi deduction remains active.

Emergency Loan Policy

  • • Eligibility: Urgent cases, approved and issued within days.
  • • Max Amount: 2,500 AED.
  • • Annual Limit: Max 1 Emergency Loan per member per calendar year.
  • • Repayment Duration: 1 month excluding remaining days of current month. (E.g.: Issued 21 Oct 2026 → Due on or before 30 Nov 2026).
✈️

Vacation Loan Policy

  • • Eligibility: Members traveling for vacation.
  • • Max Amount: 2,500 AED.
  • • Annual Limit: Max 1 Vacation Loan per member per calendar year.
  • • Advance Notice: Must request minimum 25 days prior to the loan need date.

⚙️Google Sheet API & Settings

Open Sheet ↗

Deploy the provided google-apps-script/Code.gs inside your Google Sheet as a Web App ("Execute as: Me", "Who has access: Anyone").

How to connect live to your Google Sheet:

  1. Open your Google Sheet and click Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Copy the code from the file google-apps-script/Code.gs in this repo and paste it into Apps Script.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment.
  4. Select Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  5. Click Deploy and paste the resulting Web App URL above!
